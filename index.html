When should it alert you?

Sensitivity changes the alert threshold, not the baby’s behavior or sound. High sensitivity alerts at 25, medium at 50, and low at 80.

Sound must stay at or above the threshold for 2 seconds before an alert. This setting pauses repeat notifications, not the room audio. Changes apply from the last alert.

Alert at 50

Sound 0 is below the alert threshold of 50.

Why wait between alerts?

Every alert starts your selected waiting period, regardless of sound level. When the pause ends, sound must pass a new 2-second check before another alert.

Try lowering the slider to 0 during the pause. Once it ends, the monitor returns to listening.
